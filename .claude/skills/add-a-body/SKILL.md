# Adding a body

[`docs/adding-a-body.md`](../../../docs/adding-a-body.md) is the checklist: what to answer, where it goes, and what happens if you skip it. Read it first and take every fact from there. This file carries only the order.

1. **Rehearse before acquiring anything.** Both halves of the checklist's first group answer out of module-level tables, so the whole registry surface walks before a byte of data exists. Under an hour, and the only part that gets cheaper by being early.
2. **Run the Python rehearsal, then the TypeScript one.** The first names what is unanswered; the second makes the compiler enumerate it. The checklist gives both commands.
3. **Answer the first group until it runs, then walk the second deliberately.** The first stops you. The second does not: skip one and it still renders, and a render that is wrong looks like a render.
4. **Take the third group last.** Nothing in it asks, nothing in it is a registry, and how much a third body inherits there is unmeasured.

Two things to hold on to while working:

- **Anything ratified by eye is Rohan's, not a default.** The ramp, the ice white and the terrain exaggeration all reach the globe.
- **Do not price the work off Mars.** The month it cost went on making the seams body-agnostic, which is paid and inherited. `docs/PROCESS.md`'s planet columns price a body's pass, never its arrival.
